# Creative Strategy Hall of Fame

> 50 people worth studying if you want to become unusually good at creative strategy. This is a curated learning canon, not an objective 1–50 ranking.

Creative Strategy inherited ideas from direct response, account planning, copywriting, art direction, brand strategy, behavioral science, marketing science, conversion, and modern performance creative.

Use this page to answer: **Whose brain should I borrow for the problem in front of me?**

## Selection Standard

A person belongs here when their documented work materially changed how strategists understand customers, build briefs, develop ideas, position brands, test creative, interpret evidence, or run creative organizations. Popularity alone is not enough.

## Start With These 10

This is a learning route, not a ranking: **Claude Hopkins · Bill Bernbach · David Ogilvy · Stephen King · Jane Newman · Dan Wieden · April Dunford · Byron Sharp · Rory Sutherland · Dara Denney.**

# I · Direct Response + Advertising Foundations

These people built the logic underneath modern performance creative: **sell something, test it, learn, improve.**

## 1 · Claude Hopkins

**Archetype:** Scientific advertiser  
**Study:** Scientific Advertising; reason-why copy; testing  
**Why:** Made testing and strategic preparation central to advertising.  
**OS stages:** Goal · Hypothesis · Test · Learn

[Study Claude Hopkins →](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/claude-clarence-hopkins/)

## 2 · John Caples

**Archetype:** Direct-response copy + testing  
**Study:** Tested Advertising Methods; headline testing; controls  
**Why:** Treated every ad as something that should compete against a proven winner.

## Source Discipline

The title **Hall of Fame** here means “part of this Vault's curated learning canon.” It does not mean every person is an inductee of the American Advertising Federation or The One Club Creative Hall of Fame.

For historical claims, prefer the linked institutional biography. For active practitioners, treat their frameworks as practitioner methods unless independently validated.
